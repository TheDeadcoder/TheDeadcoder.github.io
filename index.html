Résumé

I study how AI agents fail, and how we can tell when they do. My research spans AI for Software Engineering and SRE, LLM post-training, and AI safety. I work at the point where language models stop being demos and start being systems people depend on.

I am a Lecturer in Computer Science and Engineering at BRAC University and hold a BSc from BUET. I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027 and welcome research collaborations.

GeoQL environment

Natural-language map requests to OverpassQL

A hermetic environment and benchmark for turning natural-language map requests into OverpassQL queries. Each query runs on a frozen OpenStreetMap world and on synthetic witness worlds built so that near misses return different objects, and it passes only if it matches an accepted reading on all of them.

Observes
A map request, the world it is about and, when needed, a map view
Acts
Writes one query, or first makes up to 10 tool calls: run a query, resolve a place, look up tags
Reward
1 if the submitted query passes on the real world and on every witness world, else 0
Benchmark
27 tasks over 4 real worlds and 54 witness worlds, with a public leaderboard

A reference agent that copies the right object ids scores 100% when graded on the real map alone, and 0% once the witness worlds are graded too.

DC-Ops environment

Datacenter operations on OpenEnv

A physics-based simulator of a small datacenter for training LLM agents as operators, built on OpenEnv. Cooling units, UPS batteries, a diesel generator and a transfer switch respond to every command, and each incident decides for itself when it is resolved.

Observes
A text NOC dashboard: the alert, every unit, temperatures and power readings
Acts
One of 14 operator commands per step, such as diagnose CRAC-3
Reward
Six parts every step: thermal and power safety, efficiency, progress, procedure and action quality
Scenarios
6 incidents from easy to hard: overcooling, cooling failures, a UPS alarm, a generator test and a utility outage

Used to train a 7B model with imitation and GRPO. It learned to act instead of waiting, but never resolved a hard incident.
